LOCALIZATION UPDATE - vipirc 0.7.41
2026-11-19

Base: vipirc 0.7.40 and onekeyirc 0.1.39.
Catalog: 326 terms per language, ten languages and English fallback.

COMMANDS-XX.txt reference supplements; exact C help coverage audit.

bidirectional terminal layout and native-speaker proofreading are pending.
