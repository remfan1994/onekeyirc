command /background: 0/13 catalog lines
  EN ONLY: Detaches the running session after restoring the terminal. The shell receives this application's numbered resume command.
  EN ONLY: From the shell, --sessions lists this program's attached and detached private jobs; --resume resumes its sole available detached job.
  EN ONLY: Use --resume NUMBER when more than one job exists. NUMBER is the job ID, not a process ID; it stays stable until exit and may then be reused.
  EN ONLY: --detach-session NUMBER releases an attached terminal/controller but keeps the worker, IRC connections, drafts and history alive for --resume NUMBER.
  EN ONLY: --close-session NUMBER ends the whole job, all IRC connections and transfers, and its unsaved drafts and in-memory history. Success is reported only after verified completion.
  EN ONLY: If resume is busy after a lost SSH terminal, list jobs, detach that job, then resume it. See /help sessions for the recovery sequence.
  EN ONLY: An unresponsive worker or a legacy worker lacking a requested control can fail. Installing a binary does not update a running worker; restart it deliberately after an upgrade.
  EN ONLY: Resume redraws retained in-memory scrollback even when no chat arrived while detached. /set single_session on refuses a second background job so bare --resume remains unambiguous.
  EN ONLY: To start without a terminal, use --background from the shell; see --help for this program's options.
  EN ONLY: When background_away is on and this profile permits automatic away, /background or managed terminal loss queues the configured AWAY after detach. Resume clears only that client-generated away state; a manual /away is preserved.
  EN ONLY: Add --reconnect to retry transient connection failures. See /help deployment and /help reconnect.
  EN ONLY: No terminal multiplexer is required. A pending resolver or credential helper can require a retry.
  EN ONLY: Suspending an ordinary foreground process pauses its networking. A resumed job can suspend its foreground controller while its managed worker continues networking.

  EN ONLY: Each saved server profile appends to CONFIG_PATH.log. The final parent directory must be owned with mode 0700; the file must be owned, regular, single-link and exactly 0600. Symlinks and parent traversal are refused.
  EN ONLY: Archive a full log outside the active path, then toggle logging off/on to resume. Rotation or rewriting can invalidate the previous catch-up anchor; send a new eligible message before relying on a later absence's disk catch-up.

  EN ONLY: /catchup can use a healthy log only when its exact locally sent anchor was recorded in that running session. It never asks the bouncer to fetch an archive and does not import old logs after a process restart.
  EN ONLY: Regular disk I/O can delay the process on slow storage. See /help catchup, /help history and SECURITY.md for the limits.
