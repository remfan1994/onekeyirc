EXIT STATUS

0     Normal completion or interactive exit.
    1     Operational failure, such as initialization, save or session control.
    2     Invalid options/configuration or no terminal for interactive use.
    130   SIGINT interrupted the welcome after terminal cleanup.
    131   SIGQUIT interrupted the welcome after terminal cleanup.

A successful --background status confirms local worker setup, not remote IRC
login. A failed session-close status means completion was not verified;
recheck --sessions because an accepted request may finish later.
